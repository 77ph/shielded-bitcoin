# Experiments

Standalone measurements that back a number in the documents, kept out of
the workspace so that they do not add their dependencies (other curves)
to every build. Each has its own `Cargo.toml`; run with
`cargo run --release` inside the directory.

| Directory | Backs | Prints |
|---|---|---|
| `twochain` | `docs/REPLAY-PROOF.md` §4.4, `docs/REMAINING.md` §2.3(b) (one native recursion level on a 2-chain) | the outer constraint count of verifying one Groth16 proof with 17 public inputs in-circuit, for BLS12-377 inside BW6-761 and for BLS12-381 inside BW6-767; with `--prove`, the outer setup, proving and verification times (the BW6-767 setup fails: its scalar field has 2-adicity 1) |
| `halo2` | `docs/VARIANT-A.md` §4 (the cost of a Halo 2 / IPA shielded proof) | proof size, proving and verification time of Zcash Orchard action bundles of 1–4 actions with the `orchard` crate, the nearest production relation to this project's transfer |
| `halo2-transfer` | `docs/VARIANT-A.md` §4.2 (S1: this profile's transfer relation in Halo 2) | the smallest k, proof size, key generation, proving and verification time of the 2-in-2-out transfer relation of `PROFILE.md` §8 expressed in Halo 2 with `halo2_gadgets` on Pallas, for one transfer and for two in one proof; `--mock-only` stops after the mock prover |
| `halo2-accumulate` | `docs/REPLAY-PROOF-A.md` §2 (verification of n variant-A transfer proofs: one by one, batched into one MSM, accumulated with the deferred claims settled at the end) | per-proof verification time in the three modes for n = 1, 4, 16, 64; the prover-side `G` cost; the accumulator size |
| `vesta-ecc` | `docs/REPLAY-PROOF-A.md` §3.3 (the first piece of a recursive verifier for variant A) | a library, not a measurement: `halo2_gadgets`' ECC chip (v0.5.0) ported from Pallas points in an `Fp` circuit to Vesta points in an `Fq` circuit, with the chip's own tests (`cargo test --release`); scalars are `Fp` elements embedded in `Fq` |
| `halo2-verifier` | `docs/REPLAY-PROOF-A.md` §3.3–3.4 (the in-circuit verifier of variant A's proofs, piece by piece; one recursive step) | `cargo test --release -- --nocapture`: the transcript gadget reproduces the native sponge's challenges (a transfer-shaped transcript fits k = 13); the capture of a real proof's transcript events and accumulated MSM; the verifier circuit over `Fq` that walks the events and checks the MSM over the Vesta chip, with the instance commitment recomputed from the statement, fitting k = 15; `cargo test --release prove_the_verifier_circuit -- --ignored --nocapture` proves it (13.1 s keygen, 4.5 s, 50 ms); `shadow.rs` recomputes every scalar of that MSM natively as the verifier does (checked term by term); `fieldside.rs` is the circuit over `Fp` that does the same with `field.rs`'s chip, fitting k = 13 (`cargo test --release prove_the_field_side -- --ignored --nocapture`: 2.5 s keygen, 0.6 s, 17 ms); `native.rs` is the Poseidon transcript for either curve (two 128-bit limbs per scalar on the Pallas side), `verifier.rs` one body included per side (`q`: Vesta proofs in `Fq`, `p`: Pallas proofs in `Fp`), and `step.rs` one step: `cargo test --release the_p_group_side_of_a_tq_proof -- --ignored --nocapture` proves the `q` group side as `T_q` at k = 15 under the Poseidon transcript over `Fp` (4.2 s, 4 960 bytes) and verifies that proof's group side in the `p` circuit, fitting k = 17; `cargo test --release one_transfer_proof_verified_recursively -- --ignored --nocapture` is the whole step: `T_p` = that group side + the transfer proof's field side bound in-circuit (202 links), proven at k = 17 as a Vesta proof opening the transfer proof's claim (47.8 s keygen, 17.3 s, 5 664 bytes, 5.8 GB peak) and decided natively (163 ms) without the transfer proof; `step2.rs` is the second step (`two_steps_close_the_loop`, with `SB_PROVE_STEP2=1` the real proofs: `T_q(2)` at k = 18 and `T_p(2)` at k = 17 on the server, the decider 1.6 s for both — the loop closed, §3.4); `batch.rs` the batch relation of §3.7 as a circuit (two slots at k = 17 over a synthetic history); `step3.rs` that relation inside `T_p` over a block the indexer replayed (`sba_indexer::testing`), its transfer slot bound to the proof's statement, k = 17 (`a_replayed_block -- --ignored`, with `SB_PROVE_STEP3=1` the real proof) |
| `halo2_proofs-patched` | `docs/REPLAY-PROOF-A.md` §3.3–3.4 | `halo2_proofs` 0.3.5 vendored, used by `halo2-verifier` through `[patch.crates-io]`: read-only accessors made public (the MSM's terms, `W`, `U`, the Lagrange generators, the key's commitments and constraint system, the queries' indices), and the accumulation step's prover change — `poly::commitment::Claim`, `create_proof_with_openings`, `verify_proof_with_openings`: an earlier proof's deferred claim `(G, u)` opened inside a later proof's multi-point argument at no cost in proof bytes; its own workspace, `cargo test` there (33 tests, the claims chain over k = 5 and 6 among them) |
| `cycle` | `docs/REMAINING.md` §2.3(a) (unbounded recursion on the MNT4-753 / MNT6-753 pairing cycle) | the outer constraint count in both directions of the cycle; with `--prove`, the outer setup and proof (killed at 9.4 GB on a 16 GB machine) |
